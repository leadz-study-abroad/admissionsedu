Eligibility

Entry requirements

Meeting minimum thresholds allows an application to be considered but does not guarantee admission. Illinois Tech uses a holistic review.

Undergraduate

Indian and international boards

  • CBSE: minimum 61% cumulative average.
  • CISCE: minimum 50% cumulative average.
  • IB: minimum subject score of 5.
  • IGCSE: minimum grade 6; A/AS Levels: minimum grade C or equivalent.
Undergraduate

English proficiency

  • TOEFL iBT: 80 or above.
  • IELTS: 6.5 or above.
  • Duolingo: 115 or above.
  • Qualifying school results may support a waiver under the published rules.
Graduate

AI and Computer Science

  • A four-year degree from a NAAC A or better institution.
  • Minimum: 60% cumulative average or a 7/Good rating.
  • Applicants may need prerequisite computer science coursework depending on background.
Graduate

Data Science

  • A four-year undergraduate degree and minimum 60% cumulative average.
  • Expected preparation includes mathematics, statistics, databases and programming.
  • Missing prerequisites may be completed after admission where permitted.
Graduate

MBA

  • A minimum three-year degree from a NAAC A or better institution.
  • Minimum: 60% cumulative average or a 7/Good rating.
  • GMAT and GRE scores are not required.
Graduate

English and documents

  • English: TOEFL 80, IELTS 6.5 or Duolingo 115.
  • Submit transcripts, CV, personal statement and at least one recommendation.
  • Some Indian graduates may qualify for an English waiver under the stated conditions.

Admissions

How to apply

Illinois Tech reviews academic preparation, programme fit and the information provided across the application. Final offers follow an admissions committee interview.

01

Submit the online application

Create an account, select your programme and pay the ₹5,000 application fee.

02

Provide official documents

Upload transcripts and the required supporting material. Graduate applicants also submit a CV, statement and recommendation.

03

Add optional material

Undergraduate applicants may submit recommendations or ACT/SAT scores; these are optional and are considered holistically.

04

Admissions interview

Shortlisted applicants are invited to interview with the admissions committee before a final offer.

2026/27 refund policy overview

The application fee is non-refundable. Tuition refunds follow the formally notified admission deadline: 100% subject to a permitted deduction at least 15 days before; 90% within 15 days before; 80% within 15 days after; 50% between 16 and 30 days after; and no refund more than 30 days after.

Eligible refunds are processed within 15 working days after a complete withdrawal request and required documentation are received. Scholarship adjustments apply before calculating the refundable amount.
