Eligibility

Entry requirements

These requirements come from the November 2025 prospectus. Final programme details will be published when applications open.

Undergraduate

BSc Data Science

  • Indian boards: 90% overall in the best four subjects, including 90% Mathematics and 85% in Further Mathematics or a related subject.
  • A Levels: A*AA including A* Mathematics and A in Further Mathematics or a related subject.
  • English: IELTS 6.5 overall with no score below 6.0, or the listed board equivalent.
Undergraduate

Economics programmes

  • Indian boards: 90% overall in the best four subjects, including 85% Mathematics.
  • A Levels: A*AA including Mathematics.
  • English: IELTS 6.5 overall, 7.0 in reading and listening, and no score below 6.0, or the listed board equivalent.
Postgraduate

Finance and Investment

  • A quantitative degree with an average of 60-70%, depending on the Indian institution.
  • Eligible backgrounds include finance, economics, accounting, mathematics, computing, engineering and related fields.
  • English: IELTS 7.0 overall with 6.5 in all skills, or the listed board equivalent.
Postgraduate

Financial Technology with Data Science

  • A numerate science degree with an average of 60-70%, depending on the Indian institution.
  • Relevant backgrounds include computing, data science, finance, engineering, economics, mathematics and related disciplines.
  • English: IELTS 6.5 overall with 6.5 in all skills, or the listed board equivalent.
Postgraduate

MA Immersive Arts

  • A degree in any discipline with an average of 60-70%, depending on the Indian institution.
  • Applicants need an upper second-class UK Honours degree or international equivalent.
  • English: IELTS 6.5 overall with 6.5 in all skills, or the listed board equivalent.

Admissions

How your application is assessed

Meeting minimum entry requirements does not guarantee an offer. Bristol reviews each application against published course criteria and may consider supporting information.

01

Submit your application

Provide academic qualifications, English-language evidence and the required supporting information.

02

Course assessment

The admissions team applies programme-specific selection criteria and may consider your personal statement and reference.

03

Additional review if required

Some applicants may be invited to interview, submit written work, take an admissions test or provide further information.

04

Admission decision

Eligible applicants are considered competitively according to course capacity, year of entry and fee status.

2026/27 refund policy overview

The application fee is non-refundable. Tuition refunds follow the notified admission deadline: 100% subject to the permitted deduction at least 15 days before; 90% within 15 days before; 80% within 15 days after; 50% between 16 and 30 days after; and no refund more than 30 days after.

Eligible refunds are processed within 15 working days after the signed withdrawal request and supporting documents are submitted. The admission fee is refundable on cancellation, and the security deposit is refundable subject to clearance of dues and completion of exit formalities.
