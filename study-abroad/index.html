COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Every study plan is different. These are useful starting points.

When should I start planning my application?

Starting early gives you more time to compare courses, prepare documents and understand admission timelines. The ideal lead time varies by country, institution and intake.

How do I choose the right country?

Consider academic fit, budget, course structure, location, lifestyle and your longer-term career direction. A good shortlist balances all of these factors.

Can Admissions Edu help with scholarships?

Our team can help you identify relevant funding routes and organise the information required for applications. Availability and eligibility depend on each provider.

Do you support visa preparation?

Yes. We help you understand the document workflow and prepare for the next steps after receiving an offer. Final decisions remain with the relevant authorities.
