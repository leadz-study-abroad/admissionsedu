A connected degree

Spend a semester in Scotland

Eligible undergraduate students can spend a semester at the University of Aberdeen in the UK during Year 2. Places are allocated on merit, while Mumbai tuition continues to apply.

Eligibility

Entry requirements

These requirements are drawn from the 2026 prospectus. Admissions Edu can review your board, subjects, grades and degree background before you apply.

Undergraduate

Business Management

  • Indian boards: 75% overall in Class XII and 80% in English.
  • A Levels: minimum BBC.
  • IB: 32 points overall with 5, 5, 5 at Higher Level.
Undergraduate

Computing and Data

  • Indian boards: 75% overall, 80% English and 75% in Mathematics or two Science subjects.
  • A Levels: BBC including Mathematics or two Science subjects.
  • IB: 5, 5, 5 at Higher Level including Mathematics or two Sciences.
Undergraduate

Economics

  • Indian boards: 75% overall, 80% English and 75% Mathematics in Class X, XI or XII.
  • A Levels: BBC with GCSE Mathematics grade C.
  • IB: 32 points overall with 5, 5, 5 at Higher Level.
Postgraduate

MSc Artificial Intelligence

  • A computing or closely related degree equivalent to UK 2:2.
  • Python, algorithmic problem solving and data structures are required.
  • India guidance: 55% from listed leading institutions, 60% from State or Central Universities, or 65% from Private or Deemed Universities.
Postgraduate

MBA

  • Indian applicants: bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 40%.
  • Experience: minimum two years of post-degree work experience.
  • International applicants need an equivalent degree and the same work-experience minimum.

2026/27 refund policy overview

The prospectus states that the application fee is non-refundable. Tuition refunds follow the notified admission deadline: 100% less permitted processing fees at least 15 days before; 90% less than 15 days before; 80% within 15 days after; 50% between 16 and 30 days after; and no refund more than 30 days after.

Eligible refunds are processed to the original payer after a written request and verification. The prospectus gives a 15-30 day processing period. The security deposit is refundable after applicable dues and clearances.
