VU Block Model

Focused learning, one block at a time

VU was the first university in Australia to teach this way and the first in the world to introduce the model at this scale.

FOCUS

One subject at a time

Undergraduates study one subject every four weeks for deeper understanding and less competing workload.

FLEXIBILITY

Three-hour classes

Classes run three days a week, helping students balance study with work and life.

CONNECTION

Workshop-style teaching

Small interactive classes make it easier to collaborate with classmates and teachers.

RESULTS

94% pass rate

VU Melbourne international students achieved a unit pass rate above 94% from 2022 to 2024.

How it works

Built around progress

  • Undergraduate: four-week blocks with one subject at a time.
  • Postgraduate: eight-week blocks, usually with two subjects at a time.
  • Multiple entry points: the calendar provides more than one opportunity to begin.
  • Active learning: practical classes replace passive lecture-only teaching.

Eligibility

Entry requirements

Admissions Edu can help confirm your board, subjects, qualifications and English-language evidence before you apply.

Undergraduate

Indian school boards

  • CBSE and ICSE: minimum 70% in Standard XII.
  • State boards: minimum 70% in Standard XII.
  • A minimum grade C in Mathematics or another STEM subject is required.
Undergraduate

IB and A Levels

  • IB: minimum 24 points with an IB Diploma.
  • A Levels: 7 points (CDD) across at least three subjects.
  • A minimum grade C in Mathematics or another STEM subject is required.
Undergraduate

English language

  • IELTS Academic: 6.0 overall with no band below 6.0.
  • TOEFL: 67 overall.
  • Applicants should check the individual programme page for current requirements.
Postgraduate

MBA (Global)

  • An Indian bachelor's degree equivalent to an Australian bachelor's degree in a similar discipline.
  • Minimum result: Second Division, 55%.
  • English: IELTS Academic 6.0 with no band below 6.0, or TOEFL 67 overall.
