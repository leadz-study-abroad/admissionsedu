Eligibility

Entry requirements

These highlights come from the 2026 prospectus. Admissions Edu can review your board, subjects, predicted grades and degree background before you apply.

Undergraduate

Computing and Game Design

  • Indian boards: 75% average across four core Class XII subjects, with at least 75% Mathematics at Class X.
  • IB: 32 points overall, no score below 4, with the specified Mathematics or Computer Science requirements.
  • A Levels: minimum ABB with Mathematics or Computer Science for computing programmes.
Undergraduate

Biomedical Sciences

  • Indian boards: 75% across four core Class XII subjects, including at least 75% Biology and Chemistry and 60% Mathematics at Class X.
  • IB: minimum 32 points with Higher Level Biology and Chemistry.
  • A Levels: minimum ABB including Biology and Chemistry.
Undergraduate

Business, Accounting and Finance

  • Indian boards: 75% across four core Class XII subjects, with at least 60% Mathematics at Class X.
  • IB: minimum 32 points overall with programme-specific English and Mathematics grades.
  • A Levels: minimum ABB plus GCSE Mathematics and English grade C/4.
Postgraduate

Computer Science MSc

  • A bachelor's degree with a minimum overall grade of 55% or institutional equivalent.
  • Designed for graduates from eligible science or engineering disciplines outside computer science.
  • Relevant professional or research experience may be considered.
Postgraduate

Accounting and Finance MSc

  • A recognised bachelor's degree with at least 55% overall and 60% in relevant modules.
  • Finance, accounting, economics, science, engineering or management backgrounds are suitable.
  • ACA, CPA, ACCA, CIMA or CA qualifications may also be considered.
All applicants

English and documents

  • Standard XII English: minimum 70% from listed Indian and international boards.
  • Applicants may need IELTS or TOEFL depending on their qualifications.
  • A statement of purpose or personal statement is required.

Campus and student life

The Bengaluru campus is planned for Alembic City, Whitefield, within the city's technology corridor and close to public transport connections.

Smart learning spaces

Flexible classrooms, digital teaching aids and collaborative hubs support discussion, problem-solving and applied learning.

Advanced laboratories

2026/27 refund policy overview

The application fee is non-refundable. Tuition refunds follow the notified admission deadline: 100% subject to the permitted deduction at least 15 days before; 90% less than 15 days before; 80% within 15 days after; 50% between 16 and 30 days after; and no refund more than 30 days after.

Refunds are made to the bank account provided by the student or parent after submission of the signed withdrawal form and required documents. Official university terms govern every refund.
